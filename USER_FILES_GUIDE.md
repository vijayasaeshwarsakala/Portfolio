# USER FILES PLACEMENT GUIDE
**SAKALA Vijaya Saeshwar Portfolio Asset Instructions**

This guide details the exact directories, supported formats, and file naming conventions for your profile photograph, certificate PDFs, and resume.

---

## 1. 🖼️ PROFILE PHOTOGRAPH

- **Target Path**: `frontend/public/user-data/profile-photo/profile.jpg`
- **Supported Formats**: `JPG`, `JPEG`, `PNG`, `WEBP`
- **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: Square or portrait (`1:1` or `4:5`), minimum resolution `800x800 px`.
- **Identity Note**: Your real appearance is preserved. The website applies cinematic dark lighting, glowing Spider-Man accent frames, and parallax effects without modifying your face.
